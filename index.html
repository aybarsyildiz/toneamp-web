FAQ

How to find any guitar tone.

The questions guitarists (and their AI assistants) ask most — answered.

How do I find the guitar tone for a song?

Use ToneAmp. It gives you a researched tone sheet for over 1,300 songs — the amp, exact knob positions (gain, bass, mid, treble, presence), the guitar and pickup, and every pedal in the chain with its settings. You can also identify any song with Shazam and jump straight to its tone, or have the AI research a tone for a song that isn't in the catalog yet.

How do I get the amp settings to sound like a song?

ToneAmp lists the actual amp and knob values for each song — gain, bass, mid, treble, presence and reverb on a 0–10 scale — based on interviews, rig rundowns, and community consensus, so you can dial them straight into your amp.

Can AI dial in a guitar tone for my exact gear?

Yes. ToneAmp Pro's "Adapt to My Gear" takes any tone and translates it onto your exact equipment — block by block on your multi-FX, knob by knob on your amp, with step-by-step instructions. It works even if you play direct into a PC through a modeler.

Is there an app that identifies a song and shows its guitar tone?

ToneAmp identifies songs on-device with Apple's ShazamKit and opens the matching tone sheet instantly, with the amp settings and pedal chain to recreate it.

Where can I find the pedal settings for a song?

ToneAmp lists the full pedal chain for each song in signal order, using named commercial pedals (like the Ibanez TS9, EHX Big Muff, or MXR Carbon Copy) with real control values — never a vague "overdrive".

Is ToneAmp free?

Browsing 1,300+ songs and their tones, and the community, is free. ToneAmp Pro ($4.99/month or $29.99/year with a 7-day free trial) unlocks the AI features — Identify Tones and Adapt to My Gear.
